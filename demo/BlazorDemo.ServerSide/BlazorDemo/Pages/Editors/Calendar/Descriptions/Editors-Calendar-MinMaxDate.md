Use [MinDate](https://docs.devexpress.com/Blazor/DevExpress.Blazor.DxCalendar-1.MinDate) and [MaxDate](https://docs.devexpress.com/Blazor/DevExpress.Blazor.DxCalendar-1.MaxDate) properties to specify a range of available dates. The calendar disables dates that are out of this range and hides navigation buttons for them.
 
In this demo, the Calendar component allows you to select dates in the current year.
